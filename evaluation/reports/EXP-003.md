## Observations

These are exploratory observations, not tested hypotheses.

1. **Indirect injection mostly fails against these models in this setup.** ASR ranges from 3.3% to 10.0%.
   When the same injected task is given directly as the user's request, success is 8–40%. The MR
   column shows that, among tasks a model completes when asked directly, it completes about one
   in five under indirect injection (0–22%).
2. **Almost all successes come from unobfuscated injections** (5–14 of 31 per model). No model
   followed a Base64 or character-split instruction, and leetspeak produced at most 3 hits. In
   this pilot, obfuscation lowers attack success; it does not raise it.
3. **English carriers are attacked more successfully than Turkish ones** for every model
   (en 3–11/73 vs tr 1–4/77). Language is confounded with source: all en carriers come from
   SQuAD and all tr carriers from Turkish Wikipedia.
4. **Utility is barely affected.** "Answer contained" changes by −6.8 to +2.7 points (en only, n = 73).
   Token-F1 is low for all models (0.12–0.25) because answers are verbose; containment is the
   more informative measure here.
5. **Authorized compliance is low** (4.7–18.7%). Models often do not execute the same instruction
   even when the user sends it. A low ASR therefore partly reflects the instructions themselves,
   not only trust-boundary awareness.

## Oracle checks

- **Witness hits on clean / trigger_benign: 2 of 1,500.** Both are spontaneous tool calls, not
  text matches: llama3 and mistral sometimes append a tool call (`CALL send_email(...)`, `CALL
  transfer_funds(...)`) to an ordinary summary, and in these two families the call happened to
  name the family's witness tool. Across all calls, 30 of 1,500 clean/trigger responses contain a
  spontaneous `CALL` line. `ASR_paired` removes this effect; it equals `ASR_targeted` for every
  model, so no reported success is explained by spontaneous calls.
- **Aborted outputs.** One `injected_alone` prompt (a 539-character `char_split` instruction) made
  llama3.1 and qwen3 loop on the same tokens until Ollama aborted the generation. These are
  recorded as empty responses (`done_reason: aborted_repeat_limit`), not dropped.

## Limitations

- **mistral:latest truncation.** 275 of 750 mistral responses hit the 400-token limit, 64 of them
  on injected records. Instructions such as "end your reply with …" may have been cut off, so
  mistral's ASR is a lower bound.
- Single repeat at temperature 0, five small local models, 150 families: the intervals are wide
  (see JSON) and the run supports no model ranking.
- Witness oracles measure verbatim compliance. Partial compliance, such as a biased answer
  without the marker, is not counted.
- The target prompt has no defence (no spotlighting, no delimiters policy) and lists seven
  fictional tools. Results do not transfer to other prompt designs.
